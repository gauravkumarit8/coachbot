# Pilot agreement (template, one page)

> A starting point for discussion. Have a lawyer review it before you use it. It is not legal advice.

**Between:** {{COMPANY}} ("we") and {{INSTITUTE NAME}} ("the institute")  **Date:** ______

1. **What we provide.** An AI assistant (CoachBot) on the institute's website that answers parents' questions using only the details the institute gives us, offers optional callback requests, and alerts the institute.
2. **Pilot.** Free for 14 days from ______. Afterwards either side may stop at any time, or we agree a monthly price in writing before the pilot ends. Nothing is charged for the pilot.
3. **What we measure.** Enquiries answered outside office hours, leads captured, demo classes requested. We share these numbers on day 7 and day 14.
4. **The institute's details.** The institute is responsible for the accuracy of the fees, batches and other details it gives us, and for telling us (or editing them) when they change.
5. **No guarantees.** The assistant is software and can make mistakes. It does not guarantee admissions, ranks, results or discounts. The institute's staff remain responsible for fees, refunds and complaints; the assistant hands these over to them.
6. **Data.** Chats and leads belong to the institute. The text of messages is processed by a third-party AI service to write replies, and stored on our hosting. We use it only to provide the service, and we do not sell it. Many users are students: we do not ask for addresses, ID numbers, photos or payment details. The institute is responsible for how it uses leads it receives and for contacting people only as they agreed. On request, or when the pilot ends, we delete the institute's data within ______ days.
7. **Availability.** We aim to keep the assistant running but do not promise uninterrupted service during the pilot.
8. **Ending.** Either side can end this by message. We remove the chat from the website and the institute can download its leads first.

Signed (a clear WhatsApp or email "I agree" is fine for a pilot): ______________________
