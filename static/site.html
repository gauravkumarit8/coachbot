Questions owners ask

Do parents have to give their phone number?

No. The assistant answers first and never makes help depend on personal details. A callback is offered only when a parent shows interest, and they can say no or press Skip at any time.

What if it does not know the answer?

It says so honestly, shares your contact options and records the question so you can add the answer later.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Hindi and Hinglish (Hindi written in English letters). It replies in the same style.

Whose data is it, and who can see the chats?

Chats and leads belong to your institute. You can see them, and we can see them when we support you. To write replies, the text of a message is processed by a third-party AI service. Read our privacy page.

How much does it cost?

Start with a free 14-day pilot and judge it on real numbers: enquiries answered after hours, leads captured and demo classes booked. We will agree a simple monthly price with you before the pilot ends.

How long does setup take?

It depends on how quickly you share your fee and batch details. Once we have them, we set up and test your assistant and tell you when it is ready.
