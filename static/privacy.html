{{product}} is an AI assistant that coaching institutes put on their website to answer questions from parents and students. It is provided by {{company}} ({{city}}). This page explains what happens to the information you type.

What we collect

  • Chat messages you type to an institute's assistant, and the replies.
  • Details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, class and city, when you ask for a callback. This is always optional. You can say no or press Skip.
  • A random chat ID stored in your browser so the chat can continue. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Technical data, such as your internet address, which our servers may record briefly in logs and use to stop abuse.
  • Contact form on our home page: the details you enter there.

The assistant does not ask for your home address, ID numbers, photos or payment details. Please do not type them.

Why we use it

  • To answer your questions and help the institute follow up if you ask for a callback.
  • To let the institute review chats and see questions its details do not cover, so it can improve.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.

Who can see it

  • The institute you are chatting with, which decides how to use the chat and any details you share.
  • Our service providers that help us run the service: an AI service that processes the text of your message to write a reply, and our hosting provider. They process data only to provide their service to us.
  • Anyone else only if the law requires it.

We do not sell your information.

Students and children

Many people who use these assistants are students. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sharing a phone number or other personal details. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete anything a child shared.
